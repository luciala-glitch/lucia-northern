# Week 5 error analysis: what limited the Week 4 model

Source: the saved Week 4 out-of-fold (OOF) development predictions (1,330 teeth, 727 radiographs) and
external-test predictions (327 teeth, 180 radiographs). All numbers are regenerated by
`python -m week5.evaluate`.

## 1. Baseline vs learned model (Week 4)

| External test | Classical baseline | Week 4 learned (Raw) |
|---|---:|---:|
| MRE (px) | 56.87 | 13.45 |
| SDR@10 px | 5.4% | 59.0% |
| ICC(2,1) PBL% | 0.351 | 0.939 |
| Weighted κ | 0.402 | 0.886 |
| Stage accuracy | 51.7% | 86.5% |

The learned detector's MRE was already 4.2× lower than the classical baseline's, so Week 5
targets the learned model's *residual* failure modes rather than the architecture choice.

## 2. Which landmarks are misidentified (Week 4 Raw, development OOF)

| Landmark | MRE px | SDR@10 | Along-axis px | Perpendicular px | Along/perp | External MRE px |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mesial CEJ | 13.09 | 53.7% | 11.84 | 3.79 | 3.1× | 11.44 |
| Distal CEJ | 12.37 | 55.5% | 10.75 | 4.28 | 2.5× | 12.52 |
| Mesial bone crest | 17.08 | 59.8% | 14.80 | 5.49 | 2.7× | 14.29 |
| Distal bone crest | 17.21 | 58.6% | 14.88 | 5.63 | 2.6× | 15.13 |
| Apex | 17.20 | 50.0% | 10.90 | 10.63 | 1.0× | 13.88 |

The **distal bone crest** has the highest MRE. CEJ and bone-crest errors lie mainly along the tooth's
long axis (along/perpendicular ratio 2.5–3.1×). That is the direction
that changes PBL%, so a crest placed slightly too coronal or apical moves the stage. The apex error is
closer to isotropic: it is harder to find, but it changes PBL less, because it only enters the denominator.

Recurring pattern: 3/5 had **orientation metadata more than 45° wrong** (the detector was
told the apex was at the wrong end), 5/5 were tilted more than 15°, and 3/5 produced an
anatomically invalid measurement. All five had very large reported σ, so these teeth were *detectable*
failures. They are not silent errors.

## 4. The three most significant sources of error

1. **Wrong apex direction causes gross failures.** 84 development teeth
   (6.3%) have orientation metadata that is more than 45° wrong. Their Week 4 MRE was
   37.7 px against 15.4 px overall. The worst
   5% of teeth by error produce 23% of all landmark error. The error
   distribution is heavy-tailed, so the mean is dominated by a few catastrophic cases.
   Week 4 trusted the reader's (or the metadata's) apex direction without checking it.
2. **Bone-crest and CEJ displacement along the long axis.** This is the clinically consequential error
   direction. It shows as 135 undercalls against
   85 overcalls on development, and
   39 Stage III/IV teeth were called Stage I/II.
3. **Uncertainty was not calibrated or stage-aware.** The Week 4 95% PBL interval covered the true PBL in only
   80.0% of development teeth (nominal 95%). Spearman ρ between
   landmark σ and error was 0.39. The shipped boundary-margin rule still
   auto-accepted 13 development Stage III/IV undercalls, because it
   measures distance to a boundary and ignores the direction of the error.

## 5. Planned improvements and outcomes

Each change was tested on development data only and kept only if it lowered cross-fitted development MRE.

| Weakness | Change tried | Why | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orientation self-check: also run the reversed-apex hypothesis; switch, and always flag for review, when the model is more certain that way | Turns silent metadata errors into flagged, mostly fixed predictions | **kept** (dev MRE 14.23 px); development-fitted ratio 1.0; switched 84 dev teeth, 70 truly mis-oriented, 14 false switches (all flagged) |
| 1, 2 | Re-train with stronger rotation/scale and gamma/blur/noise augmentation | More robust to tilt and poor films | `strong_aug` 13.30 px (Δ -0.48) — **selected** (fold-1 screen, seed-noise floor 0.44 px) |
| 2 | Higher coordinate-loss weight; sharper heatmap targets; learning rates 2.5e-4 and 1e-3 | Penalise along-axis coordinate error directly | `coord_0.1` 13.66 px (Δ -0.15); `sigma_2` 14.66 px (Δ +0.87); `lr_2.5e-4` 14.97 px (Δ +1.19); `lr_1e-3` 13.89 px (Δ +0.11) — **not selected** (fold-1 screen, seed-noise floor 0.44 px) |
| 2 | Raw/CLAHE ensemble (fixed 50/50, and learned per-landmark weights) | CLAHE sharpens the crest edge; averaging reduces variance | **kept** (dev MRE 14.83 px) |
| 2 | Multi-margin test-time augmentation (box margin 5/8/11%) | Averages out sensitivity to the reader's box | **kept** (dev MRE 14.62 px) |
| 3 | Landmark σ calibrated to empirical 95% coverage; Monte Carlo stage probabilities | Makes the interval mean what it says | see uncertainty analysis |
| 3 | Stage-aware review rule with a separate Stage III/IV miss guard | Targets the clinically dangerous undercalls | see uncertainty analysis |

The fixed 50/50 blend (cross-fitted development MRE 14.83 px) beat learned
per-landmark weights (14.92 px), so the simpler blend was kept.
