Geometric Definitions, Units, and Mathematical Formulation
Discard simple Euclidean pixel distance between arbitrary points. Dental radiographs frequently show tilt, crown inclination, and foreshortening. All distances must be projected along the tooth's anatomical long axis.

Tooth Long Axis Vector (u): Define the unit vector running from the midpoint of the crowns/CEJ line down to the anatomical apex:

u=xapex-xmid-CEJxapex-xmid-CEJ

Landmarks per side (k{mesial,distal}):

xCEJ,k=(x,y): Cementoenamel Junction

xBC,k=(x,y): Alveolar Bone Crest

xApex=(x,y): Anatomical Root Apex

Projected Distances (px):

$\text{Defect Distance } (d_{\text{defect}, k}) = \vert{}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{BC}, k} - \mathbf{x}_{\text{CEJ}, k}) \cdot \vec{u}\vert{}$

$\text{Root Length } (L_{\text{root}, k}) = \vert{}(\mathbf{x}_{\text{Apex}} - \mathbf{x}_{\text{CEJ}, k}) \cdot \vec{u}\vert{}$

PBL Percentage Formula:
$$\text{PBL}_k \,(\%) = \left( \frac{d_{\text{defect}, k}}{L_{\text{root}, k}} \right) \times 100$$

Stage Assignment: Final stage classification must be determined dynamically from the worse side: PBLmax=(PBLmesial,PBLdistal).

Stage I: $< 15\%$

Stage II: $15\% - 33\%$

Stage III/IV: $> 33\%$ (Flag for furcation/vertical defect check)

Rigorous Statistical Repeatability Analysis

Move beyond simple mean absolute difference. For your two independent measurement rounds across 15 teeth (30 root surfaces), compute:

Intraclass Correlation Coefficient (ICC(2,1)): Two-way random effects, single rater, absolute agreement for continuous PBL $\%$.

Cohen's Weighted Kappa (w): Quadratic weighting to quantify agreement on ordinal stage classifications (Stage I, II, III/IV).

Bland-Altman Analysis: Plot the difference between rounds against their mean. Calculate the Mean Bias (d) and the $95\%$ Limits of Agreement (LoA): d1.96SD.

Identify whether the measurement variance is systemic (e.g., consistent landmark drift) or random noise.

Error Decomposition & Edge-Case Stress Testing
